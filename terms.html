2. The service

CrossAware is a safety-assistance application. It detects your proximity to mapped danger zones — railway crossings, dangerous intersections and similar locations, currently in Riga, Latvia — and automatically lowers the media volume on your device and plays a short voice alert, so that you can hear approaching trains, trams and traffic. The service includes the mobile application, the crossaware.com website, the interactive demo, and related features we may add over time.
