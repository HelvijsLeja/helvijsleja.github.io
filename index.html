Headphones mask the train

Music, a podcast or a video at full volume covers the train horn, the tram bell and the car you did not see.

📱

You can't remember every crossing

Railway crossings, tram tracks and busy junctions are on every route. Reaching for your phone at the crossing is the worst moment to look away.

How It Works

How CrossAware works
in three steps

01
📍

Your phone knows the danger zones

Riga's railway crossings, tram crossings and busy junctions are loaded on your phone. Your location is checked against them on the device; it is not sent to our servers.

02
🏃

On foot or by bike only

Walking or cycling, CrossAware is on. At car speed, alerts stay off.

03
🔉

Volume drops, you hear a warning

About 15 metres before a danger zone, media volume drops and a short voice says "Be aware!". Once you are clear, volume goes back to where it was.

Features

Riga Danger Zone Map

Pre-loaded with railway crossings, tram crossings and busy intersections across Riga, based on OpenStreetMap data.

Walking & Cycling Only

Speed detection turns alerts off when you move at car speed, so they don't fire while you drive.

A short spoken "Be aware!" (English) and a notification. No need to look at the screen.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a personal safety app?

A personal safety app is software that helps protect you during everyday activities like walking, cycling, or commuting. CrossAware is a personal safety app that works automatically in the background, lowering your media volume near danger zones so you can hear approaching traffic and trains.

What's a good Life360 alternative?

If you're looking to replace Life360's family location sharing, CrossAware isn't that. CrossAware does something different: it automatically lowers your media volume and gives a short voice alert as you approach a danger zone, so you can hear trains and traffic. You can use it on its own or alongside Life360 or a similar app.

What is the best GPS tracker for kids?

GPS trackers and family-locator apps are designed to show where a child is. CrossAware adds a different layer: it automatically lowers media volume and gives a voice alert near danger zones, so a child can hear what's coming. Parents can use it alongside a GPS tracker.

Is there a Findmykids (Pingo) alternative?

If you already use Findmykids (Pingo) to see your child's location, CrossAware isn't a replacement — it's a different kind of tool. Findmykids is designed to show where your child is on the map; CrossAware automatically lowers their media volume and gives a voice alert as they approach a danger zone, so they can hear a train or car coming. The two can be used side by side.

Does CrossAware pause my music?

No. It lowers media volume to about a fifth near a danger zone and plays a short "Be aware!", so you still hear what is around you. Volume comes back when you are clear.

Is CrossAware available on iPhone?

Not yet. CrossAware is in Android beta with testers in Riga. An iPhone version is planned.

Does CrossAware store my location?

No. Your location is checked against danger zones on your phone. Our server stores only zone entry and exit events, without coordinates, and deletes them after 90 days.
