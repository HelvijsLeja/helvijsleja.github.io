The Problem
55%
of 116 recorded crashes with pedestrians wearing headphones involved a train
Lichenstein et al., Injury Prevention, 2012 · USA, 2004–2011
🎧

Headphones block critical audio cues

You can't hear oncoming trains, car horns, or warning signals when music is playing at full volume.

📞

Podcasts and videos pull you in too

A gripping podcast or a video on the move splits your attention just like loud music, and the sound still masks an approaching train.

📱

Manual pausing is unreliable

Reaching for your phone at a crossing takes attention away from the road at the worst possible moment.

🗺️

Danger zones are on every route

Railway crossings, tram tracks and busy intersections across the city. Most pedestrians and cyclists don't know them all, and safety shouldn't depend on memorising a map.

How It Works

Three steps.
Zero effort.

01
📍

We track your location

CrossAware uses GPS to know exactly where you are. Danger zone detection happens right on your device — if you create an account, your activity history is securely stored to power your safety stats.

02
🏃

We detect your speed

Walking or cycling? CrossAware is on. Moving at car speed? Alerts stay off.

03
⏸️

Your music turns down automatically

Within 15 metres of a danger zone, media volume drops automatically. Once you are clear, it goes back to where it was.

HOW IT FITS IN

CrossAware and family-locator apps
A complement, not a replacement

Searching for a Life360, Findmykids, Qustodio or Family Link alternative? Here's an honest answer: CrossAware isn't that. Family-locator apps are designed to show you where your family is. CrossAware is designed to act as a danger zone approaches — automatically lowering your media volume and giving a short voice alert near a railway crossing, so you can hear what's coming. You can use CrossAware on its own or alongside a family-locator app.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a personal safety app?

A personal safety app is software that helps protect you during everyday activities like walking, cycling, or commuting. CrossAware is a personal safety app that works automatically in the background, lowering your media volume near danger zones so you can hear approaching traffic and trains.

What's a good Life360 alternative?

If you're looking to replace Life360's family location sharing, CrossAware isn't that. CrossAware does something different: it automatically lowers your media volume and gives a short voice alert as you approach a danger zone, so you can hear trains and traffic. You can use it on its own or alongside Life360 or a similar app.

What is the best GPS tracker for kids?

GPS trackers and family-locator apps are designed to show where a child is. CrossAware adds a different layer: it automatically lowers media volume and gives a voice alert near danger zones, so a child can hear what's coming. Parents can use it alongside a GPS tracker.

Is there a Findmykids (Pingo) alternative?

If you already use Findmykids (Pingo) to see your child's location, CrossAware isn't a replacement — it's a different kind of tool. Findmykids is designed to show where your child is on the map; CrossAware automatically lowers their media volume and gives a voice alert as they approach a danger zone, so they can hear a train or car coming. The two can be used side by side.
